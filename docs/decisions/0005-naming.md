# 0005 — Naming: WordPress never leads

Status: **accepted** (from the rules)

- WordPress.org plugin guideline 17: a trademark can't be the sole or first term of a plugin slug; "X for WordPress"
  is the sanctioned form. The WordPress Foundation's trademark policy restricts "WordPress" in product and domain names.
- So: the plugin slug is `agent-ready`; the display name may be "Agent Ready for WordPress"; the repository and
  template avoid a leading "WordPress" or "WP".
- "Agent Ready" is a working name; the owner picks the final one.
